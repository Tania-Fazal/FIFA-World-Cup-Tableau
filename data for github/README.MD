# [HR Analytics Tableau Dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/views/HRAnalyticsDashboard_17819384679650/HRAnalyticsDashboard?:language=en-US&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)
![alt text](HR Analytics.png)

Interactive HR Analytics Dashboard developed using Tableau to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, job satisfaction, and department-level trends.

## Project Overview

This project provides an interactive analysis of employee data using Tableau.

The dashboard focuses on employee attrition, workforce demographics, education fields, job roles, departments, gender, age groups, and job satisfaction.


## Key KPIs

- Total Employees: 1,470
- Attrition Count: 237
- Attrition Rate: 16.12%
- Active Employees: 1,233
- Average Employee Age: 37

## Dashboard Analysis

### Department-wise Attrition

Analyzes employee attrition across different departments, including:

- Human Resources
- Research & Development
- Sales

### Employee Distribution by Age Group

Visualizes the number of employees across different age groups to understand workforce demographics.

### Job Satisfaction Analysis

Analyzes job satisfaction ratings across different job roles.

Job satisfaction is evaluated using ratings from 1 to 4.

### Education Field-wise Attrition

Analyzes attrition across different education fields, including:

- Life Sciences
- Medical
- Marketing
- Technical Degree
- Other
- Human Resources

### Attrition by Gender

Compares employee attrition between male and female employees.

### Attrition Rate by Gender and Age Group

Analyzes attrition rates by gender across different age groups:

- Under 25
- 25–34
- 35–44
- 45–54
- Over 55

## Key Insights

- The dashboard contains 1,470 employees.
- 237 employees are recorded as attrition.
- Overall attrition rate is 16.12%.
- 1,233 employees are active.
- Average employee age is 37.
- Attrition patterns vary across departments, education fields, genders, and age groups.
- Job satisfaction differs across different job roles.
